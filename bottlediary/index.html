한국어

하루를 길게 쓰지 않아도 괜찮아요. 손바닥만 한 작은 종이에 오늘의 마음을 적고, 접어서 유리병에 쏙 넣어두세요. 보관한 일기는 고칠 수 없어요. 그날의 마음 그대로 남겨두세요.

  • 종이 한 장의 일기: 펼쳐지는 작은 종이에 글을 쓰고, 그림을 그리고, 스티커를 붙여요. 종이 색 8가지, 손글씨 글꼴 5가지에 같은 날 여러 장도 자유롭게 쓸 수 있어요.
  • 접어서, 병 속으로: 보관을 누르면 종이가 사각사각 접혀 병 속으로 떨어져요. 병을 흔들거나 마개를 열면 지난날의 일기가 하나 날아올라요.
  • 달력으로 돌아보기: 일기를 쓴 날을 한눈에 보고, 날짜를 누르면 그날의 일기들을 넘겨봐요.
  • 나만 보는 일기: 서버도, 계정도 없어요. 모든 일기는 내 폰에만 있고, 비밀번호·패턴·생체 인증으로 잠글 수 있어요. 백업은 직접 내보내고 가져와요.
  • 광고도 인앱 결제도 없는 유료 앱이에요.

English

You don't need to write a lot. Put today on a little sticky note, fold it, and drop it into a glass jar. Kept notes can't be edited: they stay exactly as you felt that day.

  • A diary the size of a sticky note: type, draw and add stickers on a tiny sheet of paper, with 8 paper colours and 5 handwriting fonts. Write as many notes a day as you like.
  • Fold it into the jar: tap Keep and watch the note fold up and drop into the jar. Shake the jar or open the lid and an old note floats up to greet you.
  • Look back with the calendar: see which days you wrote at a glance, and flip through the notes of any day.
  • Just for you: no servers, no accounts. Your notes stay on your phone, behind a passcode, pattern or biometric lock if you like, and you export and import your own backups.
  • A paid app with no ads and no in-app purchases.

The purchase screen shows the price, but the payment method should be “Test card, always approves” and there should be a line saying it's a test order and you won't be charged. That's expected: tap the buy button. If it shows a real payment method instead, or doesn't say it's a test order, don't pay. Email attostudio.support@gmail.com instead. It can take a little while after you join the group before you're set up as a tester, so you can also try again a bit later.

Please keep the app installed and stay in the test and the testers group for at least 14 days, until we tell you it's over, and take updates when they come.
