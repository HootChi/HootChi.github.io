English

Summary. Folded Days ("the app") is a diary app by Atto Studio ("we") that works entirely on your phone. There are no accounts, servers, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app sends nothing to us or to anyone else. Your diaries are kept on your device (on iPhone they may also be in your iCloud or computer backup), and the app moves them off the device only when you export them yourself. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you (section 6).

1. Information we collect

None. We don't run any servers, and the app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code. The app makes no network requests; on Android it does not even have the internet permission. The app is built with open-source libraries (React Native, Expo and others, listed in Settings → Open source licenses); none of them collect or send data. The only information we ever receive is an email you choose to send us (section 6).

2. Data stored on your device

Your diaries (what you write and draw, stickers, the date and the time you kept each one, paper colour and font), the diary you are still writing, your settings and a list of the diaries you recently took out of the bottle are kept in the app's private storage on your device. The app also stores a random number created when it is installed; the app never sends it anywhere and uses it only to tell whether the app was reinstalled, so an old app lock is not carried over.

Deleting a diary removes it. Settings → Erase all notes removes every diary, the diary you are still writing and the list of recently opened diaries; your settings and the app lock stay until you change them. Uninstalling the app removes everything, except that on iPhone the app-lock data can stay in the iOS Keychain (section 3).

On iPhone this storage may be included in your iCloud or computer backup, which is handled by Apple under your agreement with Apple — we cannot access it. On Android the app opts out of Google's cloud backup, so your diaries are not copied to your Google account. To move your diaries to another phone, use Export notes (section 4).

3. App lock

  • Passcode or pattern: if you turn on the app lock, your passcode or pattern is never stored as such. The app keeps only a salted hash of it (PBKDF2) in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), on this device only, so it does not move to another phone.
  • Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint: if you turn on Settings → Use biometrics, your phone checks your face or fingerprint itself and tells the app only whether it matched. The app never receives or stores biometric data.
  • Screen protection: while the lock is on, Android blocks screenshots and screen recording of the app, and iPhone hides the app's contents in the app switcher.
  • There is no server, so a forgotten passcode cannot be recovered. If biometrics are on, you can still unlock with them and set a new passcode or pattern; otherwise the app can only be reset by erasing every diary on the device.
  • After uninstalling on iPhone: iOS keeps the app-lock data (the salted hash and the lock settings) in the Keychain after the app is deleted. No other app can read it, and Folded Days discards it the next time the app is installed and opened.

4. Exporting and importing

Settings → Export notes creates a file with all your diaries and opens your device's share sheet; you choose where it goes (for example Files, a cloud drive or a message). We don't receive it, and what happens next is governed by the app or service you choose. The file is not encrypted, so anyone who has it can read your diaries: keep it somewhere safe. Import notes reads only the file you pick in the system file picker. While exporting and importing, the app keeps a temporary copy of the file in its private storage; it deletes the imported copy right after reading it, and the exported one the next time you open the app, export again or erase all diaries.

5. Motion sensor and vibration

To let the diaries in the bottle slide and bounce when you tilt or shake your phone, the app reads the motion sensor (accelerometer) while the bottle screen is open in front of you (not while the app is locked or another screen is open). The readings are used immediately on your device and are never stored or sent. You can turn this off in Settings → Tilt & shake, and vibration in Settings → Haptics.

6. Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write (for a refund request, also your Google Play order number). If you use Settings → Send feedback, the draft also lists the app version, OS version, device model and app language; you can delete them before sending. We use them only to answer you and, if you ask for a refund, to find the order in Google Play Console and refund it; we never share them or use them for marketing, keep them only as long as needed to handle your request and delete them when you ask. Support email is received and stored in Gmail, a service of Google LLC, on Google's servers in the United States and other countries where Google has data centers.

7. Device permissions

The app asks for no permissions except one optional one: on iPhone, Face ID, which iOS asks you to allow the first time the app unlocks with Face ID after you turn on Settings → Use biometrics. It does not access your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or notifications. On Android these permissions are granted at install and give no access to your data:

  • USE_BIOMETRIC and USE_FINGERPRINT — to unlock the app with your fingerprint or face, if you turn on Use biometrics;
  • VIBRATE — for haptic feedback;
  • com.attostudio.bottlediary.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION — added by the AndroidX Core library; only apps signed with our key can hold it.

Because Folded Days is a paid app, Google Play may also add a licence check (com.android.vending.CHECK_LICENSE), with which the app asks the Google Play Store app on your device whether this copy was installed from Google Play. The app still has no internet permission.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children in particular, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Keeping and deleting data

The app gives us no data about you, so there is nothing of that kind for us to delete; support emails are deleted from the mailbox, including its trash, once they are no longer needed or when you ask. To remove your diaries, delete them in the app or use Settings → Erase all notes; to remove all of the app's data, uninstall it (on iPhone, see section 3 for the app lock), and delete any exported files and iPhone backups you no longer want.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA or Korea's Personal Information Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. Because the app gives us no personal information, we usually hold nothing about you beyond any emails you sent us, but we will answer every request.

11. Security

Data on your device is protected by your operating system's app sandbox, the app lock keeps only a salted hash of your passcode or pattern, and the app transmits nothing over the network.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date, and mention important changes in the app's update notes.

13. Contact

Atto Studio — attostudio.support@gmail.com

요약. 접어둔 하루(이하 "앱")는 Atto Studio(이하 "개발자")가 만든 일기 앱으로, 모든 처리가 이용자의 휴대폰 안에서 이뤄집니다. 회원가입이나 계정이 없으며, 개발자는 앱을 통해 이용자의 개인정보를 수집·보관·판매·제공하지 않습니다. 서버, 광고, 분석, 추적 도구가 없고 앱은 개발자나 다른 누구에게도 정보를 보내지 않습니다. 일기는 이용자의 기기에 보관되며(iPhone에서는 iCloud나 컴퓨터 백업에도 포함될 수 있음), 앱이 일기를 기기 밖으로 보내는 것은 이용자가 직접 내보낼 때뿐입니다. 이용자가 이메일로 문의하면 보내 주신 내용은 답변에만 사용합니다(제6조).

제1조 (처리하는 개인정보 항목과 목적)

개발자는 앱을 통해 개인정보를 수집하거나 처리하지 않습니다. 개발자는 서버를 운영하지 않으며, 앱에는 분석, 오류 보고, 광고, 추적 도구가 없습니다. 앱은 네트워크 통신을 하지 않으며, Android에서는 인터넷 권한 자체가 없습니다. 앱에 포함된 오픈소스 라이브러리(React Native, Expo 등, 설정 → 오픈소스 라이선스에 목록이 있음)도 정보를 수집하거나 전송하지 않습니다. 개발자가 받는 정보는 이용자가 직접 이메일로 보낸 문의뿐이며, 제6조에 따라 처리합니다.

제2조 (기기에 저장되는 정보와 보유 기간)

이용자가 쓴 일기(글, 그림, 스티커, 날짜와 보관한 시각, 종이 색, 글꼴), 쓰던 중인 일기, 설정, 최근에 꺼내 본 일기 목록은 기기 안의 앱 전용 저장 공간에 보관됩니다. 앱을 설치할 때 만든 임의의 번호도 함께 저장되는데, 앱은 이 번호를 어디로도 보내지 않으며 앱을 다시 설치했는지 확인해 예전 앱 잠금이 남지 않게 하는 데만 씁니다.

일기를 지우면 그 일기가 삭제됩니다. 설정 → 모든 일기 지우기를 실행하면 모든 일기, 쓰던 중인 일기, 최근에 꺼내 본 일기 목록이 삭제되며, 설정과 앱 잠금은 직접 바꿀 때까지 남습니다. 앱을 삭제하면 모두 삭제됩니다. 다만 iPhone에서는 앱 잠금 정보가 iOS 키체인에 남을 수 있습니다(제3조).

iPhone에서는 이 저장 공간이 iCloud 또는 컴퓨터 백업에 포함될 수 있으나, 이는 Apple과 이용자 사이의 약관에 따르며 개발자는 접근할 수 없습니다. Android에서는 앱이 Google 클라우드 백업을 사용하지 않으므로 일기가 Google 계정에 복사되지 않습니다. 다른 휴대폰으로 일기를 옮기려면 일기 내보내기(제5조)를 이용하세요.

제3조 (앱 잠금 정보)

  • 비밀번호·패턴: 앱 잠금을 켜도 비밀번호나 패턴 자체는 저장하지 않습니다. 솔트를 더한 해시값(PBKDF2)만 기기의 보안 저장소(iOS 키체인 / Android 키스토어)에 이 기기 전용으로 보관하므로 다른 휴대폰으로 옮겨지지 않습니다.
  • Face ID·Touch ID·지문: 설정 → 생체 인증 사용을 켜면 휴대폰이 직접 얼굴이나 지문을 확인하고, 앱에는 일치 여부만 알려 줍니다. 앱은 생체 정보를 받거나 저장하지 않습니다.
  • 화면 보호: 잠금이 켜져 있는 동안 Android에서는 앱 화면의 캡처와 녹화를 막고, iPhone에서는 앱 전환 화면에서 앱 내용을 가립니다.
  • 서버가 없으므로 비밀번호를 잊으면 복구할 수 없습니다. 생체 인증을 켜 두었다면 생체 인증으로 열어 비밀번호나 패턴을 새로 정할 수 있고, 그렇지 않으면 기기의 모든 일기를 지워야만 초기화할 수 있습니다.
  • iPhone에서 앱을 삭제한 뒤: 앱을 삭제해도 앱 잠금 정보(해시값과 잠금 설정)가 iOS 키체인에 남습니다. 다른 앱은 읽을 수 없으며, 앱을 다시 설치해 처음 열 때 이 정보를 폐기합니다.
