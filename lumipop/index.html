아기 하늘다람쥐 루미와 함께 흩어진 빛 조각을 팡팡 맞추고, 빛을 잃은 하늘섬을 다시 밝혀 주세요! 연쇄가 길어질수록 연출이 빨라지고, 한 단계마다 음이 올라가고, 보드 가득 빛이 터져요.

  • 화끈한 스페셜과 콤보: 4개를 한 줄로 맞추면 빛 화살, L자·T자로는 풍등, 5개를 한 줄로는 프리즘, 네모로 4개는 반딧불이 만들어져요. 스페셜 두 개를 바꾸면 10가지 콤보가 터져요.
  • 팡 게이지와 팡파레 타임: 연쇄를 이어 팡 게이지를 채우면 팡팡 타임! 빛 조각이 공짜 스페셜로 변신해요. 레벨을 깨면 팡파레 타임이 시작돼요. 남은 이동이 모두 빛 화살과 풍등이 되어 터져요.
  • 하늘섬 모험: 새벽·한낮·노을·별밤 하늘섬, 4개 월드 200레벨. 그림자를 걷고, 구름을 치우고, 비눗방울을 터뜨리고, 도토리를 내려 주세요.
  • 강제 광고 0: 배너 광고도, 레벨 사이 광고도, 게임을 열 때 나오는 광고도 없어요. 보상형 광고는 '광고 보고' 버튼을 직접 눌렀을 때만 나와요.
  • 계정도 가입도 없이 오프라인으로 즐기는 무료 게임이에요. 선택형 인앱 구매가 있고, 일부 보상은 원할 때만 보는 보상형 광고로 받을 수 있어요.

English

Match scattered pieces of light with Lumi, a baby sugar glider, and bring the light back to the sky islands! Chain reactions speed up as they grow, every step of a chain climbs the musical scale, and the board bursts with light.

  • Explosive specials and combos: match 4 in a row for a Light Arrow, an L or T for a Sky Lantern, 5 in a row for a Prism and a 2×2 square for a Firefly. Swap two specials together for 10 different combos.
  • Pop meter and Fanfare Time: keep the chains going to fill the Pop meter. When it's full, Pop Time turns pieces into free specials. Clear a level and Fanfare Time begins: every move you have left goes off as a Light Arrow or Sky Lantern.
  • A sky-island adventure: 200 levels across four worlds, the Dawn, Noon, Sunset and Starlight Isles. Lift shadows, clear clouds, pop bubbles and bring the acorns down.
  • No forced ads: no banner ads, no ads between levels, no ads when you open the game. Rewarded videos play only when you tap a "Watch ad" button.
  • Free to play, offline, no account. Optional in-app purchases, and some rewards can be earned by watching an optional rewarded video.

Coming soon. Want to play it first?

Lumi Pop is coming to iPhone, iPad and Android. Right now it's in a closed test on Google Play (Android only), and joining takes two steps.

  1. Join the tester group: tap "Join group" at groups.google.com/g/lumipop-testers. Use the Google account your phone's Play Store is signed in with. You won't see any posts in the group — that's expected.
  2. Join the test: joining the group alone isn't enough. With that same account, open the test page and tap "Become a tester". Then tap "Download it on Google Play" on that page and install Lumi Pop. It can take a few minutes after you join the test before you can install it.

Please keep the game installed, and don't leave the test or the tester group until we let you know the test is over (at least 14 days). After that, you can leave the group, and you can leave the test from the same test page. Send feedback or bugs any time to attostudio.support@gmail.com.

Only Atto Studio can see the group's member list, which shows your Google account's email address, and we use it only to run this test.
