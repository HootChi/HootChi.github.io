English

Summary. Pangka ("the app") is a camera app by Atto Studio ("we") that works entirely on your phone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app sends nothing to us or to anyone else. Your photos stay on your device unless you save or share them yourself. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you (section 5).

1. Information we collect

None. We don't run any servers, and the app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code. The app makes no network requests; on Android it does not even have the internet permission. The iPhone app contains no third-party code. The Android app includes open-source libraries from Google (AndroidX/Jetpack) and JetBrains (Kotlin), and none of them collect or send data. The only information we ever receive is an email you choose to send us (section 5).

2. Data stored on your device

The in-app album (the photos you take or import in the app, their thumbnails and your rolls) and your settings are kept in the app's private storage on your device. They are removed when you delete them in the app or uninstall the app; photos you already saved to Photos (iPhone) or your gallery (Android) stay there. On iPhone this storage may be included in your iCloud or computer backup, which is handled by Apple under your agreement with Apple — we cannot access it. On Android the app opts out of Google's cloud backup, so the in-app album is not copied to your Google account.

3. Camera and photos

  • Camera: used only while the app is open, to show the viewfinder and take photos. The app does not record video or sound and does not use the microphone.
  • Saving: on iPhone the app asks for add-only access to Photos the first time it saves a picture. On Android it saves to your gallery (Pictures/Pangka) through the system media store, without any storage permission. You can turn automatic saving off in Settings.
  • Reading: the app cannot read your photo library. When you import a photo, you pick it yourself in the system photo picker and the app receives only the photos you picked. They are processed on your device.
  • Location: the app does not access your location, and the photos it saves contain no location information. If you import a photo that has location information, the Pangka copy does not keep it.

4. Sharing your photos

When you share a photo or a contact sheet, your device's share sheet opens and you choose where it goes. We don't receive it; what happens next is governed by the app or service you send it to.

5. Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use them only to answer you, never share them or use them for marketing, keep them only as long as needed to handle your request and delete them when you ask. Support email is received and stored in Gmail, a service of Google LLC, on Google's servers in the United States and other countries where Google has data centers.

6. Device permissions

Camera, Photos (add-only) and notification access are all optional; you can change them at any time in your device settings.

  • Camera — to take photos. Without it you can still import photos and give them a Pangka look.
  • Photos, add-only (iPhone) — to save your photos to Photos.
  • Notifications — asked when you first send a roll to develop, to tell you when it is ready (on Android 12 and earlier there is no prompt: notifications are on unless you turn them off in your device settings). They are scheduled on your device; there is no push server.

The app also uses haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. On Android, AndroidX libraries built into the app add a few permissions automatically: WorkManager, which schedules develop notifications, adds WAKE_LOCK, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED lets it re-schedule a notification after the phone restarts), and AndroidX Core adds com.attostudio.pangka.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION, which only apps signed with our key can hold. Because Pangka is a paid app, Google Play also adds a licence check (com.android.vending.CHECK_LICENSE) with which the app asks the Google Play Store app on your device whether this copy was installed from Google Play. These permissions are granted at install, give no access to your data, and the app still has no internet permission. The app does not access your contacts, microphone or location.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children in particular, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Keeping and deleting data

The app gives us no data about you, so there is nothing of that kind for us to delete; support emails are deleted from the mailbox, including its trash, once they are no longer needed or when you ask. To remove the app's data, delete photos in the app or uninstall it (and remove it from your iPhone backups if you wish).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information or Korea's Personal Information Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. Because the app gives us no personal information, we usually hold nothing about you beyond any emails you sent us, but we will answer every request.

10. Security

Data on your device is protected by your operating system's app sandbox. The app transmits nothing over the network.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date, and mention important changes in the app's update notes.

12. Contact

Atto Studio — attostudio.support@gmail.com
