한국어

팡카는 바디를 고르면 카메라가 통째로 바뀌는 디카 카메라예요. 오늘 밤엔 롤 모드로 24컷을 찍고, 내일 아침 현상된 컨택트시트를 받아 보세요.

  • 바디 8종, 한 번 결제로 전부: 피치 05부터 파티 플래시, 나이트 800까지, 바디마다 색감뿐 아니라 플래시 광량, CCD 해상감, 입자, 블룸이 달라요. 바디를 바꾸면 카메라 섀시 색도 함께 바뀌어요.
  • 롤 모드, 아침에 현상: 24컷을 찍는 동안은 결과를 볼 수 없어요. 현상을 맡기면 아침 9시(급행은 1시간 뒤)에 알림이 오고, 번호가 찍힌 필름 컨택트시트가 완성돼요.
  • 주황 LED 날짜 스탬프: 7세그먼트 LED 그대로의 날짜 각인이에요. 날짜 형식을 바꾸거나 날짜를 직접 지정할 수도 있어요.
  • 앨범 사진도 팡카로: 사진을 불러와 8개 바디를 한 번에 비교하고, 강도를 조절해 저장해요. 날짜는 원본 촬영일로 찍혀요.
  • 구독·광고·계정이 없어요. 팡카는 어떤 데이터도 수집하지 않고, 사진을 어디로도 보내지 않아요.

  3. 설치: 테스트 참여 페이지에서 Google Play에서 다운로드를 눌러 팡카를 설치해 주세요. 테스트에 참여한 뒤 설치할 수 있게 되기까지 몇 분 걸릴 수 있어요. English

Pangka is a digicam camera where picking a body changes the whole camera. Shoot a 24-frame roll tonight and wake up to a developed contact sheet.

  • 8 bodies, one price: from Peach 05 to Party Flash and Night 800, each body has its own colour, flash falloff, sensor softness, grain and bloom. Switch bodies and the camera chassis changes colour too.
  • Roll mode, developed by morning: 24 shots, no peeking while you shoot. Send the roll to develop and get a notification at 9 AM (or in an hour with express), then share a numbered 35mm contact sheet.
  • Orange LED date stamp: a true seven-segment LED date back. Change the format or set any date.
  • Bring your photos: import any photo, compare all 8 bodies at once, dial the intensity and save. The stamp uses the photo's original date.
  • No subscriptions, no ads, no accounts. Pangka collects no data and never sends your photos anywhere.

Coming soon. Want to try it first?

Pangka is coming to Android first, then iPhone. There's a closed test on Android right now. Pangka is a paid app, but during the closed test, members of the tester group can install it free as a test order.

  1. Join the tester group: with the Google account your phone's Play Store is signed in with, open groups.google.com/g/pangka-testers and tap Join group (you don't need to get group emails). Tap the profile picture at the top right of the page to check which account you're using, and switch if needed.
  2. Join the test: open the test page on your phone and tap Become a tester with the same Google account (check the profile picture at the top right again).
  3. Install: on the test page, tap Download it on Google Play and install Pangka. It can take a few minutes after you join the test before you can install it. In the Play Store, tap the button with the price to open the purchase screen. Only tap the buy button if the payment method is “Test card, always approves” and it says it's a test order and you won't be charged. If you don't see both, or it asks you to add a payment method, don't pay. Right after you join the group, the test card may not show up yet. Try again a bit later, and if it still doesn't show, email attostudio.support@gmail.com.

Please keep the app installed, and don't leave the test or the tester group until we let you know the test is over (at least 14 days). Send feedback, bugs or questions to attostudio.support@gmail.com.

日本語

パンカは、ボディを選ぶとカメラごと変わるデジカメアプリです。今夜はロールモードで24枚撮って、明日の朝、現像されたコンタクトシートを受け取りましょう。

  • 8つのボディ、一度の購入ですべて：ピーチ 05からパーティーフラッシュ、ナイト 800まで、色だけでなくフラッシュの光量、CCDの解像感、粒子、ブルームまでボディごとに違います。ボディを変えるとカメラのボディカラーも変わります。
  • ロールモード、朝に現像：24枚、撮影中は結果を見られません。現像に出すと朝9時（特急なら1時間後）に通知が届き、番号入りのフィルムコンタクトシートが完成します。
  • オレンジのLED日付スタンプ：7セグメントLEDそのままの日付です。形式の変更や日付の指定もできます。
  • 手持ちの写真もパンカに：写真を読み込んで8つのボディを一度に比較し、強さを調整して保存できます。日付は元の撮影日で入ります。
  • サブスク・広告・アカウントなし。データは一切収集せず、パンカが写真をどこかへ送ることもありません。
