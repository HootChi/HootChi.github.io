English · Daily Cut: One Photo a Day

One photo a day, one consistent tone. Turn your moments into a photo calendar, a film strip, and monthly or yearly JPEG posters.

  • One photo per date, a square crop, and a short note.
  • Six tones applied across your collection.
  • Monthly and yearly posters, plus 7/14/30-day film-strip exports.
  • Home Screen widgets, optional local reminders, and Files backups.
  • One-time paid app. No account, ads, in-app purchases, or subscription.

Photos and notes are handled in the app on your device. User-chosen Photos, Files, and sharing services manage their own copies. Backups are not encrypted.
